Install List Do

Install on your phone for full-screen offline use

On iPhone / iPad (Safari):

1 Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
2 Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" .
3 Tap Add at top right. List Do is now installed on your home screen!

On Android (Chrome / Edge / Samsung):

1 Tap the three dots menu (⋮) at top right.
2 Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3 Confirm "Install" when prompted.
